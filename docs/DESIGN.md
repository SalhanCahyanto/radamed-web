## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the rigor, sterility, and structural dependability of medical-grade engineering. Tailored for hospital directors, procurement officers, clinical engineers, and healthcare facility managers across Indonesia, the visual tone bridges technological precision with corporate stability.

The design movement is **Clinical Corporate Modernism**—a fusion of structured architectural layouts, disciplined typography, and sterile cleanliness. It communicates zero-tolerance reliability, institutional authority, and engineering precision.

### Key Visual Principles
- **Architectural Clarity:** Layouts rely on structured 12-column geometry, razor-sharp alignment, and hairline separators rather than heavy shadows or decorative fluff.
- **Sterile Whitespace:** Generous breathing room mimics high-standard clinical environments and modern surgical-radiological suites.
- **Utilitarian Elegance:** Information hierarchy is calibrated for fast scanning of technical specifications, certification badges, and operational service metrics.
- **No Extraneous Ornamentation:** Avoid teal medical clichés, neon gradients, floating glow effects, and aggressive pill shapes. Depth is created strictly via structural lines, pure white cards, and calibrated blue-gray tints.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, clinical hierarchy rooted in deep maritime blues, crisp technical slates, and immaculate white canvases.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Brand Blue (`#04467A`):** Core engineering brand signifier. Used for high-intent primary buttons, active interactive states, key iconography, and critical focal points.
- **Secondary Accent Blue (`#1E5C94`):** Mid-tone blue for secondary interactions, hovered states, table column accents, and contextual badges.
- **Deep Navy Container (`#0B2343`):** High-authority anchor color reserved for macro containers, the persistent enterprise navigation bar, conversion bands, and the global institutional footer.
- **Dark Headings / Logo Accent (`#000000` / `#0F172A`):** Pure black for high-impact display numbers and logo typographic balance; Deep Slate (`#0F172A`) for authoritative headings and high-contrast section titles.
- **Light Blue Surface (`#EAF1FC`):** Sterile tinted background used for category chips, technical metadata pills, feature icon enclosures, and active state highlights.
- **Pale Background (`#F5F8FD`):** Alternating full-width section canvas providing subtle contrast between stacked content bands without harsh boundaries.
- **Clean Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas for device cards, clinical case study modals, data sheets, and elevated content containers.
- **Subtle Border (`#DCE5F0`):** 1px hairline border tone for dividing data rows, framing cards, and structuring architectural grids.

### Functional Status Indicators
- **Success / Operational (`#0E7044` / Tint: `#E6F4EA`):** Equipment calibrated, maintenance completed, compliant.
- **Warning / Inspection Required (`#B76E00` / Tint: `#FEF3D6`):** Calibration due, parts under inspection.
- **Critical / Emergency Repair (`#B42318` / Tint: `#FEE4E2`):** Machine down, critical repair request dispatched.

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric structure with high-density data readability.

- **Primary Headings (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Features sharp geometric apertures and crisp terminals. Used for page titles, value propositions, and section headlines. Large display sizes utilize slight negative tracking (`-0.02em`) to produce a tight, confident mechanical presence.
- **Body & Data (`Inter`):** Selected for its neutral legibility, tall x-height, and superior rendering of numerical figures and technical tables. All service logs, device datasheets, warranty clauses, and form inputs utilize `Inter`.
- **Technical Specs (`Inter` uppercase 12px, 600 weight, `+0.05em` tracking):** Reserved for electromedical device category labels, serial markers, calibration tags, and table header rows.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a disciplined 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content container width of **1200px** on desktop screens.

### Grid & Breakpoint Specifications
- **Desktop (1024px – 1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter, centered with safe margins of at least 32px (`2rem`). Content elements span 3, 4, 6, 8, or 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px (`1.25rem`) gutter, 24px (`1.5rem`) outer margin. Side-by-side technical specs drop to 4-column pairing.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutter, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margin. Complex multi-column tables convert to vertical card stacks.

### Section Vertical Rhythm
- Full section alternating breaks utilize `space-3xl` (96px) on desktop and `space-2xl` (64px) on mobile.
- Interior module spacing strictly adheres to the 8px grid increment scale (`space-xs` to `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve clinical hygiene and precision engineering rigor, this design system avoids heavy, blurred, or colored drop shadows. Visual depth is established through **architectural layering and hairline framing**.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` or alternating `#F5F8FD` background. No elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structured Cards & Grids):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1px solid #DCE5F0` hairline border. No shadow in static state.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Interactive Card):** Remains framed in `1px solid #DCE5F0`, paired with an ultra-subtle architectural shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(4, 70, 122, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(11, 35, 67, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns & Modals):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #DCE5F0`, supported by a clean offset shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 35, 67, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(11, 35, 67, 0.06)`.
- **Dark Tier Containers:** `#0B2343` blocks create absolute structural contrast without drop shadows, using internal `#DCE5F0` dividers at 10% opacity (`rgba(220, 229, 240, 0.1)`).

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on a measured, calibrated corner curvature: **8px corner radius (`roundedness: 2`)** across standard interactive and structural components.

### Radii Rules
- **Buttons, Text Inputs, Select Fields:** Fixed `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Technical Containers, Data Panels:** Fixed `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Tags, Status Chips, Micro Pills:** Standardized to `4px` or `6px` (`0.25rem` – `0.375rem`) to maintain architectural stability rather than organic capsules.
- **Icons & Badges Enclosures:** `8px` rounded rectangles with `#EAF1FC` background fills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#04467A` background, pure `#FFFFFF` text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 600 weight), 8px border radius, 0px border. Hover state switches to `#1E5C94`. Active state: `#0B2343`. Height: 44px (Medium), 52px (Large).
- **Secondary (Outlined):** Transparent background, `1.5px solid #04467A`, `#04467A` text. Hover state: `#EAF1FC` surface fill with no change in border color.
- **Dark Surface CTA:** For buttons inside the `#0B2343` container: Solid `#FFFFFF` background, `#04467A` text. Hover state: `#EAF1FC` background.

### 2. Input Fields & Dropdowns
- **Default State:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #DCE5F0`, 8px border radius, text color `#0F172A`, placeholder `#64748B`. Internal padding: 12px horizontal, 14px vertical.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#04467A` with a crisp `2px` focus halo in `rgba(4, 70, 122, 0.15)`.

### 3. Equipment & Service Cards
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background, 8px border radius, bordered with `1px solid #DCE5F0`.
- Padding: `24px` (`space-lg`).
- Equipment photo sits inside an internal `#F5F8FD` background box with a 4px inner radius to maintain clinical hygiene before card details.

### 4. Chips & Status Badges
- **Specification Chip:** `#EAF1FC` background, `#04467A` text, 4px border radius, 4px vertical / 10px horizontal padding, 12px uppercase font.
- **Operational Status Chip:** Left-aligned 6px circular indicator dot inside a soft tinted tag (`#E6F4EA` for operational, `#FEF3D6` for scheduled maintenance).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Unchecked:** `1.5px solid #DCE5F0`, `#FFFFFF` interior, 4px border radius for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- **Checked:** Solid `#04467A` background with crisp white icon checkmark.

### 6. Domain-Specific Components
- **Calibration & Compliance Badge:** Compact data box pairing a green tick or shield icon with the calibration certificate number, expiry date, and BPFK/Kemenkes compliance status.
- **Service Request Status Tracker:** Multi-step horizontal progress bar detailing: *Request Logged &rarr; Biomedical Diagnostic &rarr; Spare Part Procurement &rarr; Repair & Testing &rarr; Recalibrated & Delivered*. Filled nodes use `#04467A`, awaiting nodes use `#DCE5F0`.
- **Engineering Specification Table:** Alternating row stripes (`#FFFFFF` and `#F5F8FD`), separated by `1px solid #DCE5F0` horizontal lines, featuring monospaced metric figures (kL, mA, kV, MHz) in `Inter`.
